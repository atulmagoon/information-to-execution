    Strategy

    Build vs buy

    A starting view for a working session, not a decision
    Layer 1 · The core principle

    Build where you win. Buy what you need to run. Own what you can't afford to lose.

    WinCapabilities that create competitive edge.
    RunCapabilities every firm needs equally.
    Can't afford to loseOur data, the evaluation of our models, client trust. Own these whichever way we go.
    Layer 2 · Six dimensions to evaluate
    1

    Strategic value

    • Does it touch our edge: investment judgement, proprietary data, unique process?
    • Would a competitor on the same tool erase our advantage?
    High value leans build
    2

    Capability and fit

    • Is there a mature vendor that fits without heavy customisation?
    • Can we do it better than the market, and keep doing it?
    Strong vendor fit leans buy
    3

    Economics

    • Build: engineering plus 3–5 years of upkeep, talent and infrastructure.
    • Buy: licences, integration, switching costs and renewal increases. Plus the opportunity cost of the team.
    Compare 3–5 year total cost
    4

    Risk and control

    • Can portfolio, deal or client data leave our environment? Auditability, explainability, Singapore data residency.
    • Vendor stability, lock-in and concentration; SOC 2, ISO 27001, access controls, model governance.
    Sensitive data leans own
    5

    Speed and timing

    • How urgent is the need? Buying usually wins on time to value.
    • In AI a build can be obsolete in 12 months; vendors absorb that churn.
    Urgency leans buy
    6

    Organisation and adoption

    • Will users adopt it? Vendors bring polish; internal tools fit the workflow tighter.
    • Who owns it long term? Does building grow a skill we want in-house?
    No owner, don't build
    Layer 3 · Applied to this workflow
    5 build · 25%15 buy, configure or reuse · 75%

    The builds cover what cannot be outsourced: proprietary data, the investment thesis, proof the AI works on our models, institutional memory and trade protection. The analyst owns the valuation model. This demo builds everything only to show the flow; the real split would be agreed with investment, technology, risk and compliance.
